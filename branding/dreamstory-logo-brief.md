# DreamStory logo — first-round design brief

## Summary

DreamStory is a local-first AI novel-writing workbench for authors who want one calm place for story planning, world
building, chapter writing, and long-term continuity.

## Audience

Primary: independent novelists and serious hobbyist writers who care about ownership of their work, continuity, and a
focused writing environment. They will meet the mark in the desktop/web workbench, app or favicon surfaces, exported
manuscripts, and project folders.

## Brand direction

**Feel like:** calm, literary, imaginative, dependable, deliberate.

**Avoid feeling:** childish, noisy, generic AI/SaaS, mystical-by-default.

**Promise:** a private, steady place where a story can be carried from its first idea through every revision.

## Competitive/category notes

Developer-tool and AI-product marks often cluster around abstract shapes, blue/purple gradients, containers, chat
bubbles, and literal spark/robot imagery. Writing-product marks often reach for books, quills, pages, or moons. The
open territory is a quiet, editorial symbol with a single ownable gesture: a thread, aperture, or protected loop.

The bundled reference-library check found direct dream/moon precedents (`dreamhost.svg`, `moon.svg`) and direct story/
book precedents (`storybook-icon.svg`). Those cues are treated as references to avoid copying, not as directions to
follow.

## Word map

- **Dream:** threshold, quiet, inner world, night, aperture, glow. Avoid a literal crescent as the primary idea.
- **Story:** thread, arc, chapter, continuity, path, seed, return.
- **Local-first:** kept, home, shelf, ownership, protected, offline. Express through containment/continuity rather than
  a literal house or lock.
- **AI companion:** branching, spark, prompt, possibility. Avoid a robot, brain, or chat bubble.

## Mark-type hypothesis

Explore three deliberately different types: a letterform/negative-space `DS` for recognition and app-icon strength; an
abstract open loop for a story that can keep unfolding; and a reduced pictorial seed for warmth and imagination. A
combination mark can be built after a direction is selected.

## Initial concept longlist and score

Scores are 1–5 for clarity, distinction, simplicity, relevance, and small-size strength. The three selected concepts
are the ones with the strongest combination and the most useful contrast.

| Idea | Type | Score | Decision |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Threaded DS: a D-shaped frame crossed by one continuous S thread | letterform / negative space | 22/25 | Build |
| Quiet Orbit: an open loop around a steady story core | abstract | 20/25 | Build |
| Story Seed: a single seed/idea opening into two narrative threads | pictorial / abstract | 20/25 | Build |
| Page Threshold: a page corner reduced to a doorway shape | pictorial | 18/25 | Hold |
| Chapter Spine: three chapter bars joined by a single spine | abstract | 18/25 | Hold |
| Private Shelf: a contained mark with a protected inner gap | abstract | 17/25 | Hold |
| Dusk Cursor: a D-shaped aperture with one writing cursor | letterform | 17/25 | Hold |
| Return Arc: one loop that returns to its starting point | abstract | 17/25 | Hold |
| Folded S: an S made from a single page-fold ribbon | letterform | 16/25 | Hold |
| First Spark: a restrained four-point story prompt | pictorial | 15/25 | Drop for now; too close to an AI cliché |

## Assumptions to confirm later

- The existing dark ink / paper / teal interface palette is a useful starting point, not a fixed identity constraint.
- `DreamStory` is the final public spelling and should remain visible in the primary lockup.
- The symbol needs to work as a favicon, desktop/app tile, social avatar, and one-colour print mark.
- No trademark clearance is implied by this exploratory round; a professional trademark and reverse-image search remains
  necessary before launch.

## Feedback incorporated — round 2

The first round was judged too abstract: it did not make the connection to writing a novel without an explanation. The
second round therefore promotes manuscript lines, chapter pages, and the writing cursor to primary visual evidence.
The goal is not a literal book clip-art mark, but a sign that an author would recognise as a writing environment at a
glance.

The next direction was clarified further: the primary visual association should be the book itself. Round 3 therefore
tests direct but reduced book silhouettes rather than cursor, abstract arc, or document metaphors.

The following iteration intentionally avoids presenting multiple near-identical book icons. It tests one distinct
brand-led idea: a book cover/page block containing a custom quotation mark, so “book” and “novel writing” arrive
together in one compact sign.
